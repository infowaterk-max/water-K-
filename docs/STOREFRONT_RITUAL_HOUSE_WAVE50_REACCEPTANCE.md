- Beauty & Wellness family structural distinctness;
- shared Story + Visual layered hero composition;

- 7/12 gallery + 5/12 buybox responsive PDP contract;

- product-truth and commerce authority boundaries;
- editorial mood/ritual navigation remaining outside medical and psychological authority;
- provider-neutral shared E13 checkout;
- no release-side mutations.

## Authority boundaries

Ritual House remains an editorial merchandising direction:

- mood is merchant taxonomy, not psychological-state inference;
- ritual is editorial navigation, not a health protocol;
- E7 only presents supplied structured scent, format, ingredient and product-attribute evidence;
- E10 remains editorial Story content;
- price, inventory, variants, reviews and product facts remain authoritative shared bindings;
- checkout remains shared provider-neutral E13.

No diagnosis, treatment, aromatherapy efficacy, sleep/stress/anxiety scoring, medical advice or psychological assessment authority is introduced.

## Implementation rule

The inherited `ritual-house.ts` is intentionally unchanged at wave start. Template-local hardening is allowed only if the current-baseline Wave 50 gate proves genuine drift. Shared runtime allowlists, component registries and binding namespaces must not be widened to make the template pass.

## Non-scope

No duplicate Ritual House template, no Visual Builder implementation, no template-local layout/hero/guidance/product/pricing/inventory/checkout/payment engine, no SQL/customer-baseline migration, no Supabase mutation, no K&H/vPOS behavior change, no production deployment, no `main` merge, no tenant status/plan change and no Wave 51 implementation.
