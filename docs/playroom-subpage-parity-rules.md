# Playroom subpage parity rules

Status: acceptance discipline for the Playroom v19 reference subpages.

The accepted Home is the visual authority for **density, proportion and hierarchy**, not only palette. A subpage is not in parity merely because it uses the same navy/cyan/magenta colors.

## Core rule

A subpage must feel like a task-focused continuation of the Home. It should reuse the Home's compact panel rhythm, neon accents, deliberate information density and controlled media scale while giving priority to its own job.

## Media scale

- Subpage media is supporting content, not a second Home hero.
- Catalog/discovery hero media should normally stay around 16–20rem on desktop.
- Editorial feature media should normally stay around 18–22rem on desktop.
- Product gallery may be larger because it is commerce-critical, but its first view must remain compact enough that decision data is visible without an image dominating the page.
- Secondary/story images should normally stay around 14–18rem.
- If a secondary image has more visual weight than the accepted Home hero, the composition is suspect.

## Density

- Prefer one compact section containing media + task controls + small signals over a giant split hero followed by empty space.
- Use micro-panels for platform, stock, compatibility, next actions, reading metadata and related paths.
- Reduce vertical whitespace before adding decorative content.
- A page may be calm, but it must not look unfinished.

## Archetypes

### Catalog = discovery

Compact intro, immediate platform/discovery controls, real facets and a denser real product grid. Decorative media must not push products below the fold unnecessarily.

### Product = commerce decision

Product gallery, buybox, stock/price/options and compatibility evidence are the authority. Secondary story media is smaller than the purchase area. Facts and compatibility should be visible as structured panels, not separated by large empty bands.

### Blog article = editorial reading

Compact editorial header, readable content column, useful aside/next actions and related paths. The article image supports the story rather than becoming the entire page.

## Data authority

Visual parity must never replace:

- catalog products and facets;
- product gallery, price, inventory, variants or purchase action;
- compatibility evidence;
- article title, summary, body or image bindings.

Fallback/demo content is allowed to make visual QA representative, but live authority stays bound to the existing shared commerce/content read models.

## Acceptance gate

Do not propagate this grammar to the remaining Playroom pages until Catalog, Product and Blog Article are visually accepted together with Home. Once accepted, derive Search/Cart/Checkout/Account/Content/FAQ/Contact/Legal/404 from the closest archetype instead of inventing new one-off page grammar.
